# Instructor Notes

This is the transition module from the dev type focus of the software craftsmanship weeks to the enterprise operations week.

One of the requirements for this week is that no new technology stacks should be introduced, so you way want to refer back to some of the stacks referred to, like microservices, to provide context.

The initial section rectifies the traditional imbalance on DevOps most classes have where most of the emphasis is on the "Dev" and only cursor mention is made of the "Ops" part.

Systems thinking section is just a high level overview but systems thinking concepts will be referred to in the rest of the week.

I suggest that some of the material in the resources folder be used for deeper dives.

The Business Impact Analysis section is pretty much standard and based on the official standards from the two industry standard associations

## The Labs

I have used these labs in the past to help students transition their intuitive sense or ideas of risk and resilience to thinking about those concepts in a more operational enterprise context.

I often have the students do group discussions and presentations on some of the case studies and exercises. Sort of a mental warmup and brainstorming sessions.

Use them however you think the class would benefit.

**Don't feel obligated to use all the labs, just choose what you think would be appropriate for your class, and  modify as you see fit**

You might also consider seeing what the sort of recent IT disasters are out there you can bring into class for a discussion.
