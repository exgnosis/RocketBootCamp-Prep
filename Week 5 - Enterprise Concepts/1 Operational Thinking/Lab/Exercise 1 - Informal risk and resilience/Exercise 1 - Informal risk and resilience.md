# Exercise 1: Risk and Resilience Thinking

Risk and resilience based thinking is not a matter of following rules or procedures, but is a mindset. A mindset is characterized by a way of looking at a situation and how observations are interpreted.

The purpose of these initial exercises is to show how the way we deal with operational issues, failures, resilience and recovery in IT systems is an application of general approach to handing these issues in other fields and areas of management and engineering

## Part 1: Risk and Resilience Planning

This first exercise is a mindset exercise. 


For context, consider the 1998 Ice Storm that paralyzed parts of eastern Canada and the north-east United States.

[1998 Ice Storm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_1998_North_American_ice_storm)

[1998 Ice Storm](https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/ice-storm-1998)



### Task #1:

You are in charge of emergency disaster response for the Canadian government
- Review the material provided in the links
- You are tasked with 
  - Developing a risk management plan (very informal and high level) for the government agency you now run
  - Developing a recovery plan to get regions affected tack to full operations
  
This is intended to be an informal exercise. What you need to consider:
- What are the risk indicators that you would observe to identify a potential ice storm?
- What are the points of failure. Consider an ice storm to be an attacker - what is vulnerable. Prepare a high level risk analysis of what the specific risks (for example, power outage) and evaluate in terms of risk probability and potential impact. Risk matrices are useful here,
- What preventative measures could you take to reduce the risk or impact for each?

The second dimension to consider is how to recover from a future ice storm.
- Conduct a high level business impact analysis on the effect of an ice storm on a populated area. 
  - Adapt the ideas of RTO and MBCO and MTPD to this scenario.
  - You don't need to identify quantitative values, just show how these ideas covered in class can be applied here
- Come up with a high level recovery plan to get the affected regions back up to normal operations.



### Task #2

You are the owner of a grocery store that was affected by the ice storm.
- Develop a risk management plan and recovery plan for your store
- Follow the same ideas as in task #1 but now the scope is from the perspective of a small business owner.

### Task #3

Redo the exercise but now from the perspective of a parent at home with small children.
- Follow the same ideas of task #1 but now from the perspective of parent looking out for their family.
