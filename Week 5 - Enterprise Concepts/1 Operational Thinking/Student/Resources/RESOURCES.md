# Systems Thinking, Causal Loop Diagrams, and Feedback Loops

### LOOPY - a tool for thinking in systems
An interactive browser tool for drawing causal loop diagrams and simulating feedback
loops by sketching circles and arrows and pressing play. Excellent first hands-on
experience for students, and it can be hosted offline.

- Author: Nicky Case
- Tool: https://ncase.me/loopy/
- Source to clone or mirror: https://github.com/ncase/loopy

### MIT OpenCourseWare - System Dynamics
University courseware covering feedback loops, causal loop diagrams, stocks and flows,
and the behavior of complex systems. Includes slides, notes, assignments, and in some
cases video.


- Authors: MIT Sloan School of Management (J. Sterman, H. Rahmandad, J. Paine, and others)
- One-day workshop with video (RES.15-004):
  https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-15-004-system-dynamics-systems-thinking-and-modeling-for-a-complex-world-january-iap-2020/
- Introduction to System Dynamics (15.871):
  https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-871-introduction-to-system-dynamics-fall-2013/
- System Dynamics II (15.872):
  https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/sloan-school-of-management/15-872-system-dynamics-ii-fall-2013/


### How to do (or not to do) ... using causal loop diagrams
A practical, step-by-step how-to paper on building and using causal loop diagrams. Written
for health systems research, but the method and the worked guidance transfer to any domain.

- Source: Health Policy and Planning, via PubMed Central
- Link: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9661310/


### Open University OpenLearn - Causal loop diagrams
A short, beginner-friendly unit on causal loop diagram notation, including the s and o
link signs and the B and R loop labels.

- Source: The Open University, OpenLearn
- Link: https://www.open.edu/openlearn/science-maths-technology/engineering-technology/causal-loop-diagrams

### Donella Meadows - Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System
A foundational essay on where to intervene in a system for the greatest effect. One of the
most widely used systems thinking teaching texts

- Author: Donella H. Meadows
- Link: https://donellameadows.org/archives/leverage-points-places-to-intervene-in-a-system/

### Donella Meadows - Dancing with Systems
A short, accessible companion piece on living and working with complex systems.
- Link: https://donellameadows.org/

### The Systems Thinker - Causal Loop Construction: The Basics
