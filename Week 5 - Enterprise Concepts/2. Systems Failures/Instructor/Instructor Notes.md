# Instructor Notes

This is a continuation of the previous section that provides a typology of ways systems can fail. 

One thing to set up is the relationship between failure modes and the systems thinking from the previous section.

For the lab, I usually have students do a report on the way systems fail is illustrated by the case study.

Most of the material here is from my Risk and Resilience materials so it does stress the relationship between those areas. Operations thinking is all about risk management and resilience when systems fail.

I have tried to keep the terminology and content consistent with industry standards


## Lab exercise

In their groups, students are instructed to essentially do systems forensic analysis of two well know system outages.

There is no right answer for the presentations but stress how they must be prepared to argue their case based on the facts.

After each presentation, I allow for a question and answer period where other teams can ask questions about the presenting teams findings. After all the presentations, I have each team self assess their own report and identify how they could improve it after seeing how they compare with the other presentations.
