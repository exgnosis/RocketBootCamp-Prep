# Instructor Notes

The main focus of this section is on resilience, recovery models and responses to risk.

It should provide an introduction into some of the industry standard approaches to risk and resilience in operational environment.

There is also an optional section on modern systems if you need it. This is more just background in case students have not been exposed to some of the technology.

## Lab

The lab is a case study of a famous case in the US government where which involved trying to put together a comprehensive terrorist database and information integration system.

The original project was a disaster and several following incarnations were also a disaster.

The materials provided are the official government documents, released by Members of Congress, as well as several analyses.

The lab is about assessing risks, latent faults and other aspects of the project. I find this often is well received by students because it deals with an actual real world distributed system with dire consequences if it fails.
