# Lab Instructions

In the last two labs, you worked with an imaginary and contrived case study.

In this lab, you will do something similar but with a real world system that was in use at the US Department of Homeland Security

The project TIDE (Terrorism Information Datamart Environment) was a centralized clearing house of all intelligence information on terrorists and terror related activity.

The system was an attempt to unify the work of multiple agencies, like DHS, CIA, NSA, Police etc into a single distributed operational environment.

The various watch lists and other actionable alerts used by the US and other governments were generated from TIDE.

From the start, the project was beset by problems from failures in design, implementation and operation. 

More information on the project is found int the `Tide Railhead` folder. Read all the documentation provided before beginning.

There are two parts to the lab.

### Part 1: Critical Analysis 

Based on what you have learned this week, do a critical operational analysis of the TIDE and RAILHEAD projects based on the material provided.

If you can find additional material online, use that too.

Specifically, this is a system that needs to be highly available, often producing real time results and accurate. 

Identify the operational weaknesses, risks and potential failures that are present in both TIDE and RAILHEAD (and whether RAILHEAD actually fixes these issues).

### Part 2: Recommendations.

This is where you get to be creative.

If you were asked to provide suggestions for a new system that would replace both of these, what recommendations would you make regarding operational architecture, resilience, risk and the other topics we have covered in class.
